# Quiz Master concept selection

Generated with the built-in GPT image tool. Direction 3 Discovery Gallery selected;
this replaces the earlier Direction 2 choice. The related family is now implemented.
Target: 390 x 844 logical phone viewport. Stats are illustrative, not live data.

1. Knowledge Studio: warm cream/apricot, realistic book/bulb hero, Science feature card and four compact topic cards.
2. Quiz Show: midnight violet/gold, buzzer hero and five stacked topic rows.
3. Discovery Gallery: porcelain/cobalt, exhibit-style category grid and full-width Technology card.

Current feature contract: five offline categories (Science, History, Geography, Mathematics, Technology), question-count setup, quiz gameplay, inline answer explanations, results and exit confirmation, help, local best scores and played count. Post-quiz answer review was approved and is implemented. No new modes or difficulty promised.

Nine related mockups are ready; inventory, full prompts and composition notes are in
RELATED_SCREENS.md. The family and isolated artwork are now implemented; see
docs/QUIZ_MASTER_REDESIGN.md. Keep all text/state native in Flutter. Compact devices reduce
decorative hero first; these are visual concepts, not verified responsive implementations.

Friendly sophisticated collectible physical science objects, bevels and restrained shadows. Avoid crowns and trophies in hero.
