# GameVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

GameVerse is provided by NexDark Labs. Contact: nexdarksolutions@gmail.com.

## Offline gameplay and information collection

GameVerse works offline and does not require an account. This version does not send gameplay records or personal information to NexDark Labs. It contains no advertising, analytics, cloud leaderboard, in-app purchases or tracking SDKs. Fonts, artwork, questions and sound effects are bundled with the app.

## Information saved on your device

GameVerse saves game statistics, high scores, achievements, saved matches and preferences on your device. These records are used to resume games and display your progress. No name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access is requested for gameplay.

## Your choices and retention

You can reset statistics in games that offer a reset control. Clearing GameVerse's app data in Android settings removes local app records and preferences. Uninstalling normally removes app data, but Android or your device's backup service may retain or restore it according to your device settings. NexDark Labs does not operate that backup service or receive its backups. The app's vibration permission is used only for optional game feedback.

## Contacting us and external services

The Contact Us control opens your chosen email app. If you email nexdarksolutions@gmail.com, we receive the email address and information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive information. You can request deletion of correspondence by contacting the same address. Your email provider and Google Play process information under their own privacy policies; those services are separate from offline gameplay.

## Children

The offline app does not collect personal information from children or other players. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy questions. If personal information concerning a child is included in correspondence, please contact us to request its deletion.

## Changes and contact

This policy describes the current offline version. If future features change data practices, we will update the policy and applicable store disclosures before releasing those features. For questions about this policy, contact NexDark Labs at nexdarksolutions@gmail.com.
